Decoding Microbial Life with AI & Metagenomics
</h1>
<p class="text-base sm:text-lg text-slate-600 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed font-normal mb-6">
My research lies at the intersection of <strong class="font-semibold text-slate-900 dark:text-slate-100">computational biology, machine learning, and microbiology</strong>. I develop and apply deep learning models to explore massive metagenomic datasets, with the goals of describing planetary viral diversity and elucidating functions of novel genes.
