Contributor to scikit-bio, a fundamental Python library providing efficient algorithms for sequence analysis, phylogenetics, diversity metrics, and statistical methods. Published in **Nature Methods** (2025). Serves as the backbone for QIIME 2 and many bioinformatics tools.
