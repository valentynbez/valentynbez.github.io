abstract: "Primary central nervous system lymphoma is a rare neoplasm, exceptionally responsible for developing a frontal lobe syndrome. A 71-years-old patient with primary central nervous system lymphoma had undergone frontotemporal craniotomy for tumor removal. Month later, showing syncopies, cognitive impairment, and visual hallucinations, the patient was admitted to our psychogeriatric ward. On admission, the patient presented delusional symptoms and disorientation, and was agitated and aggressive. This case illustrates the diagnostic and therapeutic challenges of delirium superimposed on dementia in the context of brain malignancy."
